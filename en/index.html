I build software when failure isn't an option.

Today I run a distributed team of 25 senior engineers spread across time zones we don't confirm publicly. No office. No client logos on the homepage. We have non-disclosure agreements and a track record we only discuss in private.

How we work

On demand. On your terms, never.

We accept work only under very selective terms. Here's the process.

01

Confidential request

You tell us about the project under NDA. No public forms, no open inbox.

02

Fit assessment

We evaluate whether the problem is worth the team. Most requests aren't.

03

Closed-scope proposal

If we accept, we assemble a dedicated team and lock scope, timeline and terms — no subcontracting.

04

Classified execution

Encrypted communication, verifiable deliverables, and zero public mentions unless you authorize it.

Fewer than 3% of the requests we receive become a project.
